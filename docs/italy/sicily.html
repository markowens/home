Possible Rome Itinerary

In Italy, authorized taxis are white in color and must have “Taxi” written on the roof. They must be equipped with a fare meter that indicates the real-time cost of the journey, to which extra costs for luggage, public holidays, night travel and extra-urban routes (as is often the case for journeys to airports) must subsequently be added. To book a taxi, you can go to one of the designated areas marked by yellow lines or an orange sign, or call the radio taxi numbers, which vary from city to city.

Single, pay-as-you-go taxi number – 892192 – is active throughout the country, which sorts requests to more than sixty radio taxi companies, covering about 70% of the available vehicles in Italy. In some cities, it is also possible to book a taxi via apps that you can download to your smartphone.

Always be wary of anyone offering taxi services on arrival in the city or at the airport or station: these may be unauthorized taxi drivers who should be avoided. In Italy, taxis have easily identifiable dedicated area and drivers do not go around looking for customers.
