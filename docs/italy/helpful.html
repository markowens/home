Arrival Instructions

Upon our arrival in Rome, Italy, we'll go through Passport Control, then pick up our luggage. Once you get your bags, wait so we can all go through Customs as a group. DO NOT leave the baggage claim Area and go through Customs on your own!

Calling the U.S.A

It is strongly recommended that you discuss with your cell phone provider a plan that will enable you to have international calling capabilities. It is imperative that you can be reached, or you can reach others throughout the duration of the tour. If you wish to call somebody in your contacts using your mobile device, you only need to go to your contacts and call – there is no need to dial “001”.

Time Difference

Italy is 6 hours ahead of the Eastern United States.

Currency

The EURO is the currency used in Italy.
One EURO (€ 1.00) = $1.16 Dollars

Some banks charge a flat fee for changing money plus a percentage fee. Credit card transactions in most cases will give you the best rate of exchange. Also, ATM machines are available, and you can obtain local currency by using your ATM card at most locations. In most instances, you will get the best exchange rate when you use your ATM card to withdraw money. Check with your bank regarding ATM fees and/or foreign transaction fees.

Note: If you are prompted by the ATM machine to ACCEPT conversion or DECLINE conversion, always decline. Also, if a merchant asks you when you are using a Credit Card if you want to pay in Dollars or Euros, always pay in EUROS.

Shopping

Souvenir shops can be found everywhere in Italy. Look for leather goods, ceramics, hand-made crafts, jewelry.

Credit Cards

Major credit cards such as American Express, Master Card and Visa are accepted in most shops, restaurants, and hotels. Check with your Credit Card company regarding foreign transaction fees.

If you plan to use Credit Cards for purchases, notify your Credit Card Company that you will be out of the country and will probably be using your credit card for “out-of-the-ordinary” purchases.

Travel Documents

US citizens are only required to possess a valid passport that is good at least past 6 months from the completion of the trip.

Meals

Most meals will be eaten as a group and are included in the price of the tour. You will have the opportunity, though, to venture out and have some meals on your own. Most restaurants have limited outdoor seating and offer everything from hamburgers and fries, to pizza, pasta, meats, and fish.

Please let me know if you have any special requests regarding food (Vegetarian, etc.).

Clothing

The weather is still warm in October with temperatures in the 80’s. Even though it doesn’t rain much this time of the year, it is wise to be prepared with a raincoat or an umbrella.

Clothing (Days)

Skirts, shorts, short-sleeved tops and light clothing are recommended. Hat and/or sunglasses, walking shoes or comfortable sandals are a MUST. Sunscreen/suntan lotion is also necessary.

Clothing (Evenings)

Semi-casual: skirts, dress, shorts, long-sleeved cotton. A light jacket or shawl could come in handy. Most restaurants do not require a dress code. Note: With weather pattern changes we have been experiencing lately, it is advisable to be prepared for the possibility of maybe cool evenings.

Airline Tickets

All tickets are e-tickets. You will not receive paper tickets.

What to Pack

As you prepare for your vacation, keep in mind two key words: Travel Light. Baggage handlers are not allowed inside any customs area, therefore it will be necessary for you to handle your own baggage until you are outside of restricted areas.

Before you pack, lay out the clothes you want to take. Coordinate main pieces which can be mixed and matched, need little care and are comfortable. Once you've reviewed your selection, you should be able to put half of the clothes back in the closet!!! Also remember that NO ONE is going to remember what you’re wearing. You CAN wear some items more than once. You need to be happy & healthy to fully enjoy the experience.

While all dining is generally informal, ladies may want to take a nice outfit-skirt or sundress, and men long pants. When it comes to fashion, anything goes. However, most places of worship require female visitors to cover "upper arms", and short shorts for either sex are considered inappropriate. Above all, leave your new shoes at home and bring comfortable walking shoes!

Other helpful things to pack: Sunglasses, lotion, hat, wash cloth (few hotels have them), voltage converter with adaptors.

Personal Bags: take a small purse with only a credit card, ATM card, health insurance card, and some cash. Backpacks are handy, but they’re also a prime target for pick pockets.
MEN - your wallet should always be in your FRONT pant pockets.
WOMEN - a small purse with compartments keeps your valuables safe. DO NOT have your passport, or wallet laying visible when your purse is opened.
JEWELRY Again, minimal is best. Take 2 or 3 favorite pairs of earrings and pack them in your carry on.
DO NOT pack valuables in your checked bags. The less you take, the more room you’ll have to buy new pieces and gifts for others!

Most hotels have safes to keep jewelry, passports, etc. You decide if you want to carry your passport w/ you daily. MAKE SURE you have a copy of your passport photo & NOT keep it with the original.

Value Added Tax (VAT)

Value Added Tax is levied on most articles purchased in most European countries, and overseas visitors may apply for VAT refund. A "refund voucher" must be obtained and must be presented with the goods for certification by a Customs officer at the airport of departure. The maximum refund, and the procedure for receiving reimbursement, varies by country.

Electrical Current

Since current in most countries is 220V or 240V, you will need a plug adapter and voltage converter for your curling iron, hairdryers, etc. Many hotels provide adapters and converters upon request, but it is advisable to take your own.

Health Tips

Prepare a "Health kit" containing the following suggested items (these are just some suggestions/guidelines):

  • Wash cloth
  • insect repellent
  • sunscreen
  • lip balm
  • aspirin
  • throat lozenges
  • foot powder
  • extra pair of glasses or contact lenses
  • lens solution
  • copy of lens prescription
In addition, if you are currently taking medication, bring a sufficient supply to last through your entire trip in your carry-on bag. Should you have a chronic medical problem or a history of allergies, have your doctor prepare a statement or letter outlining your condition and required medication.

Remember that we’ll be experiencing jet lag, new foods, climates and cultures. Be good to yourself. Stay hydrated & try to get sufficient sleep every night. Flying is dehydrating. Take any & all liquids offered on planes. Try to carry bottled water with you on tours.

Always hand carry any necessary medicines - do not pack them in your checked suitcase! Also, it is advisable to take enough medicine to last a few days past your return date in case you are delayed or “stranded” at an airport or another country!

Please remember that this is a “tour”. Walking is done over uneven surfaces, and cobblestone pavement. Expect most of the sightseeing to require walking as well as considerable time standing. Many cities have specific driving regulations, which require hotel and tour drop off and pick up points several blocks from the hotel or the site, that result in extra walking to and from the sites and hotels.

Baggage Allowance

Baggage allowance on transatlantic flights is one (1) checked bag, one (1) carry-on bag, and one (1) personal item (see below):

Checked Bag: 1 bag up to 23 kg (50 lbs)* with outside dimensions (L+W+H) not to exceed 62 inches. (The Tour Operator and Bus Company strongly recommend that you DO NOT exceed 40 lbs.)
Carry-on: 1 piece - not to exceed 18 lbs.
Personal item: Purse, laptop bag, or similar.

You are allowed 1 checked and 1 carry-on (plus purse/laptop/small bag) at no extra charge. Pack extra clothes in your carry-on. As you know, sometimes you and your checked luggage may be separated, and it could take a few days before you “reunite” again!!!

If you have an American Airlines Frequent Traveler number, give it to the attendant at the Check-In counter or enter it if you check in online.

Mobile Passport Control (MPC)

If you would like to expedite the Passport Control process when returning to the United States, you may want to consider “Mobile Passport Control”. Mobile Passport Control (MPC) allows eligible travelers to submit their travel document, photo, and customs declaration information through a free, secure app on their smartphone or other mobile device. The use of MPC streamlines the traveler’s entry process into the United States by reducing passport control inspection time and overall wait time. Use of MPC does not require pre-approval. Travelers who successfully use the MPC app will no longer have to complete a paper form or use an APC kiosk. As a result, travelers may experience shorter wait times, less congestion and efficient processing. You can obtain additional information by visiting their website at Mobile Passport Control.
