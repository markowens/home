Calling the USA

It is strongly recommended that you discuss with your cell phone provider a plan that will enable you to have international calling capabilities. It is imperative that you can be reached, or you can reach others throughout the duration of the tour. If you wish to call somebody in your contacts using your mobile device, you only need to go to your contacts and call – there is no need to dial “001”.
