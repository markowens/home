Trip Overview

Once In A Lifetime Ireland Tour

All aboard for a fantastic 15 day, Ireland guided tour. Circle the entire island, including Northern Ireland's coastline and the best of the spectacular Wild Atlantic Way, from Donegal to Cork.

Embark on our longest Ireland tour itinerary ever. Enjoy a gentle tour pace and double night stays in the charming rural towns of Donegal, Westport, Killarney, and Parknasilla.

Explore the stunning Causeway Coast, followed by the rugged beauty of Mayo, Galway, Clare, the famous Cliffs of Moher and the natural wonderlands of Connemara, Kerry and The Burren.
