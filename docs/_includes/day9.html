Day 9 — Friday, October 9

Summary: Travel to the hilltop town of Monreale to visit the Norman Cathedral, an architectural masterpiece, with its interior of glittering 12th century Byzantine mosaics. Return to Palermo for a tour of the ancient Old City with its incredible mix of architectural styles, including its magnificent Cathedral. In the afternoon, discover Palermo on your own with its bustling street markets, winding alleyways, and colorful back streets.

Activities

  • Travel to the hill town of Monreale.
  • Visit the Norman Cathedral.
  • Admire the glittering 12th-century Byzantine mosaics.
  • Guided tour of Palermo's Old City.
  • Free afternoon to explore markets, cafés, and historic streets.
