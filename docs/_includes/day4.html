Day 4 — Sunday, October 4, 2026

Activities

Summary: Depart Rome for Catania, Sicily. Arrive in Catania and visit the lava-crusted slopes of Mt. Etna, dominating the scenery. Standing at 11,000 feet above sea level, it's the most active volcano in Europe. Ascend the Mount to reach the Silvestri Craters; step carefully on the varied layers of lava which have solidified over the centuries. Learn about Etna's impact on the surrounding area and its unique geology.

For the Etna excursion, we will first take the cable car from around 1,900 meters (6,200 feet) up to 2,500 meters (8,200 feet). From there, we continue by 4x4 vehicle to around 2,800 meters (9,200 feet), and then we’ll do a short, guided walk with a volcanological guide, reaching approximately 3,000 meters (9,800 feet). The walk is not a long or difficult hike, but the terrain is volcanic, with black volcanic sand, rocks and uneven ground. Because of this, I recommend comfortable hiking shoes or sturdy sneakers with good grip. Hiking boots are not strictly necessary if you have good walking shoes/sneakers, but I would avoid smooth-soled tennis shoes.

Please also bring a light jacket or windbreaker, as it can be much colder and windier at 3,000 meters (9,800 feet) than in Catania.

The exact conditions and accessible altitude can vary depending on weather and volcanic safety conditions, but I’ll make sure you have all the information on the day.

One thing I would recommend is wearing comfortable, sturdy shoes, as some parts of the trail can be rocky and uneven, with loose stones. Also, if anyone in the group has high blood pressure or any other condition that could be affected by walking or altitude, it’s important to take it easy, stay hydrated, and let me know beforehand so we can take any necessary precautions.

Nothing too demanding, but it’s always better to be prepared and enjoy the experience safely! I’m sure we will enjoy!
