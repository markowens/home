Day 6 — Tuesday, October 6

Summary: Depart for Syracuse. Here, you can step back in time to the bygone era of Greek Sicily and explore the birthplace of Archimedes. Visit Neapolis Archaeological Park, the Greek Theater, the remains of Roman Amphitheater and the "Ear of Dionysus," an awe-inspiring artificial limestone cave renowned for its outstanding acoustics. Then, admire its old quarter, a UNESCO World Heritage site, located on the island of Ortygia before continuing to Ragusa/Ebla.

Activities

  • Depart for Syracuse.
  • Visit Neapolis Archaeological Park.
  • Visit the Greek Theater.
  • See the Roman Amphitheater.
  • Visit the famous Ear of Dionysus cave.
  • Admire its old quarter, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, located on the island of Ortygia
  • Continue to Ragusa/Ebla.
